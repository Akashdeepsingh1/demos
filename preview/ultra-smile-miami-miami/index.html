Questions, answered

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans and handle the paperwork for you. Call us and we'll verify your coverage before your visit — no surprises.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes — toothache, chipped tooth, lost filling: call us and we'll get you seen the same day in most cases.

I'm anxious about dental work. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book and we'll go at your pace, explain everything, and keep you comfortable.

Do you see children?

Yes — first visits are all about making kids comfortable, and we keep them that way.
